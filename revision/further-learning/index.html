Further learning

The books the course is built on, and video channels worth watching alongside these notes.

Reference books. These are the core IET books for the course. Make sure you have the editions that match the current amendment, BS 7671:2018+A4:2026. Prices were checked in November 2026 and will change.

  • BS 7671:2018+A4:2026, Requirements for Electrical Installations (the IET Wiring Regulations, 18th Edition). The standard itself, and normally the only book allowed in the 18th Edition exam. Buy: IET shop (£125) · Amazon (£115)
  • On-Site Guide, 9th Edition. Applying BS 7671 to straightforward installations, with handy tables. Buy: IET shop (£40) · Amazon (£36)
  • Guidance Note 3: Inspection & Testing, 10th Edition. How to inspect, test and certify. Buy: IET shop (£46) · Amazon (£36.33)
  • Electrician's Guide to the Building Regulations, 7th Edition. Part P and the other Parts that affect electrical work. Buy: IET shop (£35.50) · Amazon (£35.50)
  • Code of Practice for In-service Inspection and Testing of Electrical Equipment, 5th Edition. Testing portable and other equipment in use (PAT). Buy: IET shop (£66.00) · Amazon (£65.86)
