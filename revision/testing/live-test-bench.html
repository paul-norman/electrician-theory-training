Live test bench

Practise the live tests from the demonstrations: Ze and fault current at the origin, Zs by calculation or measurement, RCD tests, and proving supply polarity, with mistakes and faults to spot.

Key takeaways

Ze: main switch off and locked, earthing conductor disconnected (or the bonding gives a falsely low reading), neutral probe first, then line. Typical maximums 0.8 Ω TN-S, 0.35 Ω TN-C-S; above 200 Ω a TT electrode is unstable. The tester also gives the fault current, I = UZ; record the higher of PSCC and PEFC as Ipf, and check it doesn't exceed the devices' breaking capacity. Zs = Ze + (R1 + R2) is GN3's preferred method; a measured loop test trips 30 mA RCDs unless set to no-trip. RCDs: test button first, ½ × IΔn no trip, 1 × IΔn within 300 ms on both half-cycles, record the longer time.
